Feedback Mechanics
Coach Anchor Points: a few home-reality details that choices preserve or corrupt.

Coach Deviation meter (0–100), flavor-text only, gates which scene pools the remix engine draws from.

Visual & Art
Direction You Explore visual style next, staying zero-art-background friendly.
Coach Typography + SF Symbols only; deviation meter drives a calm→glitchy accent-color shift.
You Include custom art — roughly one illustration per branch-reality scene.
Coach Two shapes: reusable 8–12 "archetype" library vs. one unique illustration per branch flavor (10–15).
Decision You One unique illustration per branch-reality flavor (~10–15 total).
Decision You Generative-AI art at dev-time, pre-generated and bundled — not fetched at runtime.
Endings & Fails

Technique 2 of 4

Job To Be Done

Two "hires" for this project — the builder's and the player's — running side by side on the console. Both had to be satisfied by whatever survived scoping.

Job 01 · The Builder

Get all the way through App Store submission, and learn what it actually takes to build an iOS app.

Coach

Scope-vs-job tension surfaced: build the full concept (deviation, anchors, certainty, remix engine) as v1 — more risk before the first submission — or ship a lean MVP first and treat richer mechanics as a v1.1 update, which also teaches the update pipeline.

Decision You

Ship the lean, linear MVP first to reach submission fast. Richer mechanics deferred to v1.1.

Job 02 · The Player

See how their choices affect the story — and the story has to be compelling enough to keep them going.

Coach

A minimal "choice echo" belongs in the lean MVP itself, not deferred: later scenes reference an earlier pick by name/detail via a few conditional branches — cheap delivery of "my choices matter."

Decision You

Choice echo stays in v1. It does not get cut with the rest of the mechanics.

🎯 Branching Choices With Real Consequence

"Choice echo" — the single capability that survives if everything else is cut.

Coach

Make it unbelievably good: let a choice's consequence resurface 2–3 times across the tree instead of a single callback — a felt thread, not a one-liner.

Coach

End-of-run "memory" screen: lists the choices made and what they actually caused.

You

The memory screen also carries a score — how closely you stayed to the "ideal," home-bound path.

Coach

Lightweight v1 version: each choice gets a small +/− alignment value at write-time, summed silently through play, surfaced only as a score/tier at the end. No live meter, no theming system pulled forward.

The shape of the idea itself

Every Playthrough Is a Fork

One stranding, many timelines. The player never sees this map — it's decided silently, choice by choice, by the alignment score, and only revealed at the very end.

Home — 2–4 endings, all anchors restored before certainty runs out Stay — 1–2 endings, player deliberately lets the anchors go Limbo — the rest: partial restoration, or certainty exhausted, anchors still broken

Illustrated counts above are one representative sample inside the taxonomy's designed ranges — the exact tree shape is a v1 writing decision, not a fixed spec.

Where every branch converged

v1's whole Must-have set — choice echo, alignment score, ending taxonomy — is one idea wearing three coats:
make the story visibly respond to the player.

That throughline is also the direct answer to the session's real worry: is this story compelling enough to keep someone playing? Every surviving mechanic exists to make the answer "yes."

Ships in v1

  • Choice echo — consequences resurface 2–3× across the tree
  • Silent +/− alignment score, tallied at write-time
  • End-of-run "memory" screen with score/tier
  • Ending taxonomy: home / stay / limbo, wired off that score
  • Comedic, dark-humor hard-fail choices
  • Token "safe choices aren't guaranteed" pushback
  • ~10–15 dev-time generative illustrations, one per branch flavor

Deferred to v1.1+

  • Deviation meter & glitch theming
  • Anchor Points as a live mechanic
  • Certainty resource
  • Undo / rewind credit (+ IAP)
  • Anonymized telemetry endpoint

🍾 The bottled idea washes up here. Telemetry-for-engagement is a natural v1.1+ milestone, not a v1 need: validate compellingness informally first — self and friends playtesting the small v1 story — then add a maintained analytics endpoint later.

That's a second, deliberate revisit of the on-device-only constraint — paired on purpose with a second real iOS skill: networking, a backend, and an App Store privacy disclosure.
