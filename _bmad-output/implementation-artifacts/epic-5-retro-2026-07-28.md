## What Went Well

- **Front-loading a cross-cutting constraint change as its own epic.** Rather than deferring landscape support or retrofitting it piecemeal across Epics 2–4, the team gave it a dedicated epic and ran it immediately after Epic 1 — before any portrait-only assumptions could get baked into the reading surface. Confirmed by Brian as the standout success of this epic.
- **AD-8 held up cleanly across every follow-on story.** Once the geometry-only-vs-structural-branch distinction was written down in Story 5.2, Stories 5.3 and 5.4 followed it without needing to bend or reinterpret the rule.
- **Story 5.4 modeled first-class verification as an acceptance criterion.** AC #3 required the Simulator verification result to be recorded in Completion Notes (date + orientations checked) — directly closing the `deferred-work.md` PROCESS item from Epic 1's sprint demo, where three earlier stories had shipped with a stale "please verify" line nobody had confirmed. This is the one story in the epic where verification couldn't quietly go unreported.
- **Fast iteration via a direct UX-Designer pass (Story 5.1)** got the landscape design language written and mocked up quickly, unblocking the rest of the epic.

## Challenges

- **The Story 5.1 pipeline bypass created a traceability gap.** Design work landed directly (commit `d2b71da`, PR #28) without a story file or sprint-status entry, discovered only during Story 5.2's kickoff and code review, requiring a retroactive story file reconstructed from `.memlog.md` and the commit diff.
- **The cold-launch orientation bug lived for two epics before being fixed.** First reproduced during Epic 1's sprint demo (traced to Epic 5's own orientation-unlock work), it wasn't closed until Story 5.4 — the last story of the epic that introduced it.
- **Acceptance criteria didn't probe hard enough for downstream failure modes.** Neither Story 5.2 (orientation unlock) nor 5.3 (landscape retrofit) had an AC that would have surfaced the cold-launch first-layout-pass bug before it shipped and had to be caught manually at a sprint demo.
- **Testing expectations were implicit, not written into AC.** Where automated coverage applied (none, per AD-7's engine-only scope) and where manual Simulator verification was required were both left to Dev Notes prose rather than being acceptance criteria — Story 5.4 was the single exception, and only because a prior retro-equivalent (`deferred-work.md`'s PROCESS item) forced the issue.

## Key Insights

1. Cross-cutting constraint changes (device support, orientation, platform requirements) are cheapest to absorb as their own dedicated epic, sequenced before the work that would otherwise inherit wrong assumptions — not scattered as retrofit stories later.
2. A pipeline bypass ("go fast, skip `create-story`") is sometimes the right call, but it must stay transparent in real time — a story file and sprint-status entry created in the same session, not discovered and backfilled two stories later.
3. Verification-as-acceptance-criterion (Story 5.4's AC #3 pattern) is the fix for silent unverified "done" stories — it needs to become the default template for every story with a manual-verification surface, not a one-off reaction to a specific process complaint.
4. Deeper acceptance-criteria scrutiny at story-creation time — actively asking "what could surface after implementation that this AC doesn't already probe for?" — is the lever that would have caught the cold-launch bug during Story 5.2/5.3 planning instead of at a sprint demo.
