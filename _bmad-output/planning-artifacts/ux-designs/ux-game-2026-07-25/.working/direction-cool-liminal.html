Cool Liminal

Cool Liminal treats the dusk gradient as a threshold you're standing in, not a horror palette — violet-to-indigo suggests the exact minute between day and night when anything could still happen. The frame's diamond corner studs read like a navigation instrument; when an echo lands, the studs and the tick marks along the top edge shift from teal to a brighter cyan, as if the frame just triangulated where you've been before. Generous negative space and confident sans type keep the mood forward-leaning and precise rather than ominous.
